怎样扩大词汇量

约 186 词 · 8 句

原文

主语谓语不定式从句

从句线画在最下面，从句里再套从句，就再往下多画一条。there be 句型的主语在 be 后面。have to 和后面的动词连起来算谓语。

It’s not all that hard to build an advanced and large vocabulary. Like many things in life, it’s an ongoing process, and the best part of the process is that there’s enough room for improvement, which means you’ll just keep getting better and better. Of course you have to work at it. You wouldn’t think that a few months of exercise in your teens would be enough for the rest of your life, and that’s also true for building your vocabulary—you have to keep at it daily, and pretty soon you will find that you have an excellent vocabulary.

One of the effective ways to build vocabulary is to read good books. You need to really read at least one good book a week, preferably a classic. This isn’t as hard as it sounds, and it is far better than any other method because you improve your vocabulary while reading an interesting piece of literature. Another nice thing is that you learn both new words and their use unconsciously, meaning that you will tend to use the words learnt this way in conversations almost automatically.

意思

看中文

积累又多又高级的词汇，其实没那么难。和生活中的很多事一样，这是一个持续的过程。而这个过程最棒的地方在于，进步的空间足够大，也就是说，你会一直越来越好。当然，你得下功夫。你不会以为十几岁时锻炼几个月，就够你受用一辈子吧？扩大词汇量也是一样的道理：你得每天坚持，用不了多久，你就会发现自己的词汇量非常好了。

扩大词汇量的有效方法之一，是读好书。你得真正做到每周至少读一本好书，最好是经典作品。这并没有听起来那么难，而且比其他任何方法都好得多，因为你在读一部有趣的文学作品的同时，词汇量也提高了。另一个好处是，你会在不知不觉中学到新单词和它们的用法，也就是说，你往往会在聊天时几乎自然而然地用上这样学来的词。
