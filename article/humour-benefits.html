幽默的好处

约 116 词 · 6 句

原文

If you see humour as an optional form of entertainment, you’re missing some of its biggest benefits: Humour makes average-looking people look cute and uninteresting people seem entertaining. Studies show that a good sense of humour even makes you seem smarter. Best of all, humour raises your energy, and that can have an effect on everything you do at school, at work, or in your personal life. The increase of energy will even make you more willing to exercise, and that will raise your overall energy even more. Humour also transports your mind away from your daily troubles. Humour lets you better understand life and sometimes helps you laugh at even the worst of your problems.

意思

看中文

如果你把幽默只看成一种可有可无的娱乐，那你就错过了它最大的几个好处：幽默能让长相普通的人看起来可爱，让无趣的人显得有意思。研究表明，有幽默感甚至会让你显得更聪明。最棒的是，幽默能提升你的精力，而这会影响到你在学校、工作和个人生活中做的每一件事。精力增加了，你甚至会更愿意去锻炼，这又会让你整体的精力更加充沛。幽默还能把你的心思从日常的烦恼中带开。幽默让你更好地理解生活，有时还能让你笑对哪怕是最糟糕的难题。

要点

  1. see A as B把 A 看作 B：see humour as … 把幽默看成……
  2. optional可选的、可有可无的；option（选择）的形容词
  3. make sb look / seem + 形容词让某人看起来……；后半句省了 makes：(makes) uninteresting people seem entertaining
  4. average-looking长相普通的；average 是「普通的、一般的」
  5. a good sense of humour很有幽默感；humour 是英式拼法，美式写 humor
  6. Best of all, …最棒的是……；列好处时用来引出最重要的一条
  7. have an effect on everything you do影响到你做的每一件事
  8. be willing to do愿意做……：more willing to exercise 更愿意锻炼
  9. transport your mind away from …把心思从……带开；transport 本来是「运送」
  10. laugh at even the worst of your problems笑对哪怕最糟的难题；the worst of + 复数名词：……当中最糟的
