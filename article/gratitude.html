感恩的力量

约 130 词 · 9 句

原文

主语谓语不定式从句

从句线画在最下面。help 后面不带 to 的动词原形（reduce）也算不定式。

Gratitude is a powerful emotion and a transformative habit. It’s more than just saying “thank you”. It’s also about acknowledging the goodness in our lives and recognizing that the source of the goodness often comes from the outside of ourselves. It’s a celebration of the positive aspects of life, both big and small. Practising gratitude encourages us to change our perception from scarcity to abundance. It prevents us from focusing on what we lack or what we want, directing our attention instead to the happiness we already have. This change in viewpoint can greatly increase our life fulfillment. By encouraging joyful emotions, gratitude can help reduce feelings of anxiety and depression. Moreover, it can enhance overall mood and emotional resilience (恢复能力), enabling us to better cope with stress and life’s challenges.

意思

看中文

感恩是一种强大的情感，也是一个能彻底改变人的习惯。它不只是说一声「谢谢」，还在于承认生活中的美好，并认识到这些美好往往来自我们自身之外。它是对生活中积极一面的赞美，不论大小。练习感恩会促使我们改变看法：从「缺乏」转向「富足」。它让我们不再盯着自己缺什么、想要什么，而是把注意力转到已经拥有的幸福上。这种视角的转变能大大提升我们对生活的满足感。通过激发愉快的情绪，感恩能帮助减轻焦虑和抑郁。此外，它还能改善整体情绪、增强心理韧性，让我们更好地应对压力和生活中的挑战。

要点

  1. a transformative habit能彻底改变人的习惯；transformative 是「带来巨大改变的」
  2. more than just …不只是……
  3. acknowledge / recognize that …承认 / 认识到……
  4. Practising gratitude练习感恩；英式英语里动词拼 practise，名词拼 practice
  5. change our perception from scarcity to abundance把看法从「缺乏」转向「富足」；perception 是「看法」，scarcity（短缺）↔ abundance（充足）
  6. prevent sb from doing不让某人做……；这里是「让我们不再盯着……」
  7. what we lack我们缺的东西；what = the things that
  8. …, directing our attention instead to …逗号后接 -ing，表示「从而……」：从而把注意力转到……
  9. life fulfillment对生活的满足感
  10. Moreover, …此外，而且；前面用句号断开，后面加逗号
  11. emotional resilience心理韧性，抗挫力：受了打击也能很快恢复
  12. enable sb to do让某人能够做……
  13. cope with应对，对付：cope with stress 应对压力
