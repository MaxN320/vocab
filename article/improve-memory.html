怎样提高记忆力

约 167 词 · 12 句

原文

There is no such thing as a “bad memory”, and everyone can improve their memory, as long as you are not suffering from memory loss as medical condition. If you want to improve your memory, there are a number of things you can do.

Stop thinking that you have a “bad memory”.Too many people get stuck here and convince themselves their memory is bad so what they need to remember just slip out of their minds for some reason. What should you do? Convince yourself that you do have a good memory that will improve. And remove those negative thoughts and promise to improve your memory.

Use association to remember facts.You can create a picture in your mind to help you remember a word or an image.

Involve multiple senses.You can stimulate more parts of your brain by using as many senses as possible when memorizing information. When a larger part of your brain is active, your ability to arouse your memory will increase.

意思

看中文

世上没有「记性差」这回事。只要你不是因为生病而失忆，每个人都能提高记忆力。如果你想提高记忆力，有好几件事可以做。

别再认为自己「记性差」。太多人卡在这一步，说服自己记性不好，于是该记住的东西不知怎么就从脑子里溜走了。你该怎么做？让自己相信：你确实有好记性，而且它还会越来越好。再把那些消极的想法赶走，下定决心提高记忆力。

用联想来记东西。你可以在脑子里想象一幅画面，帮自己记住一个单词或一个图像。

调动多种感官。记东西时尽量多用几种感官，就能刺激大脑更多的部位。大脑活跃的部分越大，你唤起记忆的能力就会越强。

要点

  1. There is no such thing as …根本没有……这回事
  2. as long as …只要……
  3. suffer from …患有（某种病）；受……的苦
  4. memory loss as medical condition作为一种病的失忆；一般要加 a：as a medical condition
  5. a number of …一些，好几个（后面接复数名词）；the number of … 才是「……的数量」
  6. Stop thinking that …别再认为……；stop doing 是「不再做」，stop to do 是「停下来去做」
  7. get stuck卡住：get stuck here 卡在这一步
  8. convince yourself (that) …说服自己……，让自己相信……
  9. what they need to remember just slip …what 从句当主语，表示「他们要记的东西」；它算单数，一般写 slips，原文写的是 slip
  10. slip out of one’s mind从脑子里溜走，一下子忘了；常说 It slipped my mind. 我给忘了。
  11. for some reason不知为什么
  12. you do have …do 放在动词前面加强语气：你确实有……
  13. association联想；associate A with B 把 A 和 B 联系起来
  14. create a picture in your mind在脑子里想象一幅画面
  15. involve multiple senses调动多种感官；involve 是「让……参与进来」，multiple 是「多个的」
  16. stimulate刺激，激发
  17. as many … as possible尽可能多的……
  18. when memorizing information记东西的时候；when 后面省掉了 you are
  19. arouse your memory唤起记忆；arouse 常见的说法是 arouse interest（引起兴趣）
