Grandpa's Last Horse

爷爷的最后一匹马

　全部中文：
My grandfather was a sculptor. He lived on very little money, but he never complained. "A good sculpture starts with raw stone and a lot of faith," he often reminded me.
我爷爷是个雕塑家。他靠很少的钱过日子，却从不抱怨。「一件好雕塑，从一块粗糙的石头和满满的信念开始。」他常常这样提醒我。
One winter, the doctor told us that Grandpa's heart was weak. The tests indicated that he did not have much time left. A dark cloud loomed over our family. But Grandpa just smiled. "I still have one more sculpture to finish," he said.
有一年冬天，医生告诉我们，爷爷的心脏很弱。检查结果表明，他剩下的时间不多了。一片乌云笼罩着我们家。可爷爷只是笑了笑：「我还有最后一件雕塑要完成。」
He took on the hardest work of his life: a stone horse for the village school. Every morning, he loaded up his old truck with tools and drove to the hill behind the school. In contrast to his weak body, his hands were still strong and steady.
他接下了这辈子最难的一件活：给村里的学校雕一匹石马。每天早上，他把工具装满那辆旧卡车，开到学校后面的山坡上。和他虚弱的身体形成鲜明对比的是，他的手依然有力、稳当。
Some people suspected that he was too old to finish it. "Whether I finish it or not," he told me, "I want to try." So I decided to pull my own weight. I carried stones, cleaned his tools, and charged the battery of his work light every night.
有人怀疑他年纪太大，完成不了。「不管我能不能做完，」他对我说，「我都想试试。」于是我决定做好自己该做的那一份：搬石头，擦工具，每天晚上给他的工作灯充电。
On the last night, the horse was almost ready. Grandpa asked me to pick out the best stones for its eyes. Then he pressed a button to activate the old light, and the hill became bright like a small sun.
最后一个晚上，石马快完成了。爷爷让我挑出最好的石头给它做眼睛。然后他按下按钮，打开那盏旧工作灯，整个山坡亮得像一个小太阳。
Two weeks later, Grandpa died. The school put the horse at its front gate as a symbol of courage. Whenever I approach it, I hear his voice: "The beauty of a sculpture lies in the stone you take away." His last work demonstrates what he always believed: hard work is a part of love.
两周后，爷爷去世了。学校把石马放在大门口，作为勇气的象征。每次走近它，我都会听见他的声音：「雕塑的美，在于你凿掉的那些石头。」他的最后一件作品证明了他一直相信的事：辛苦付出，也是爱的一部分。
He never lived out his dream of becoming famous, but he lived a beautiful life. Today I am writing the preface to a small book about him. He was not a man of great intellect, but he had a great heart. The whole village treasures his horse, and one day I want to take his work even further.
他一直没能实现出名的梦想，却活出了很美的一生。今天，我正在为一本写他的小书写序言。他不是什么绝顶聪明的人，却有一颗宽厚的心。全村人都珍爱他的那匹马；总有一天，我想把他的手艺再往前推进一步。
