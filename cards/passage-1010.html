The Cake on the Street

掉在街上的蛋糕
这篇只练 7 个：2 个生词、3 个词组、2 个骨架，每个在故事里出现 2–3 次。
① 先读故事，黄色的就是这 7 个，点一下能听发音；读不懂就点「中文」。
② 看「拆开看」，注意同一个说法在故事里怎么变形。
③ 做回忆题：先自己说出来，再点空格对答案，然后点 🔊 跟读整句。
④ 最后用它们说说你自己的事。
　全部中文：
Mia was nineteen, and it was her second week at a small café. On Saturday morning, an old man came in for a big birthday cake. "It's for my wife," he said with a smile. "She is eighty today." Mia carried the cake out to his car. Then her foot hit the step, and the cake fell onto the street.
米娅十九岁，在一家小咖啡馆上班才第二周。星期六早上，一位老人来取一个大生日蛋糕。「是给我太太的，」他笑着说，「她今天八十岁了。」米娅把蛋糕端出去，往他车上送。就在这时，她的脚绊到了台阶，蛋糕摔在了街上。
That night, Mia could not sleep. She beat herself up again and again: "How could I be so careless? It was her eightieth birthday!" Her heart was beating fast, and she could not keep her fear at bay.
那天晚上，米娅睡不着。她一遍又一遍地责怪自己：「我怎么这么不小心？那可是老太太的八十大寿啊！」她的心怦怦直跳，怎么也压不住心里的害怕。
The next morning, her friend Sam called. "Don't beat yourself up about it," he said. "Everyone makes mistakes. But you can take responsibility for this one. Go and see the old man." Mia was afraid. "Being honest is hard," she said. "I know," said Sam. "But being honest is the only way to feel better."
第二天早上，她的朋友萨姆打来电话。「别为这事太自责，」他说，「谁都会犯错。但这个错，你可以担起来。去见见那位老人吧。」米娅很害怕。「说实话太难了。」她说。「我知道，」萨姆说，「可只有说实话，你心里才会好受。」
So Mia and the café owner made a new cake, and she drove it to the old man's house. At the door, she took a deep breath to keep her fear at bay. "I'm so sorry," she said. "I take full responsibility for what happened." The old man laughed. It turned out that he was not angry at all. "Sixty years ago, my wife dropped our wedding cake," he said. "We still laugh about it."
于是米娅和店老板一起重新做了一个蛋糕，她开车送到老人家里。到了门口，她深吸一口气，把害怕压下去。「真的很对不起，」她说，「发生的事，责任全在我。」老人笑了。原来他一点都没生气。「六十年前，我太太把我们的结婚蛋糕摔了，」他说，「我们到现在想起来还会笑。」
He asked Mia to stay for the party. She helped his wife cut the new cake, and the whole family sang together. It turned out that the new cake was even better than the first one. The old woman closed her eyes and said, "Mm, this is pure bliss!" Mia watched the happy faces with deep satisfaction.
他请米娅留下来参加派对。她帮老太太切了新蛋糕，一家人一起唱歌。结果新蛋糕比原来那个还要好吃。老太太闭上眼睛说：「嗯，真是太幸福了！」米娅看着一张张开心的脸，心里特别满足。
Mia still makes mistakes, but she does not beat herself up anymore. She takes responsibility for them, says sorry, and moves on. When she feels anxious, she looks for someone to help. It turns out that being kind is one of the best ways to keep anxiety at bay. Every small kindness gives her a little satisfaction, and sometimes even a moment of bliss.
米娅现在还是会犯错，但她不再狠狠责怪自己了。她会把错认下来，道个歉，然后接着往前走。感到焦虑的时候，她就去找个需要帮忙的人。原来，善良是让焦虑近不了身最好的办法之一。每一次小小的善意都会给她带来一点满足感，有时甚至是片刻的幸福。

拆开看：这 7 个

留意同一个说法在故事里怎么变形
beat yourself up词组过分自责，一直怪自己
自己「揍」自己：出了错以后，一遍遍在心里骂自己，放不下。
She beat herself up again and again.
Don't beat yourself up about it.
She does not beat herself up anymore.
⚠️ yourself 跟着人变：I beat myself up ｜ she beats herself up。
take responsibility for词组为……承担责任，把事情认下来
主动把事揽到自己身上：是我的错，我来认。
You can take responsibility for this one.
I take full responsibility for what happened.
She takes responsibility for them.
⚠️ take 后面是名词 responsibility，不说 take responsible for。
keep … at bay词组不让（不好的东西）靠近
把坏东西挡在一定距离外：害怕、焦虑、压力都能这样「挡」。
She could not keep her fear at bay.
She took a deep breath to keep her fear at bay.
… one of the best ways to keep anxiety at bay.
⚠️ 不好的东西放在 keep 和 at bay 中间；这里的 bay 不是「海湾」。
It turns out that …骨架结果发现……，原来……
本来以为是 A，后来发现是 B。
It turned out that he was not angry at all.
It turned out that the new cake was even better.
It turns out that being kind is one of the best ways …
⚠️ 讲过去的事用 turned；that 后面跟完整的句子。
Being + 形容词骨架「……这件事」当主语
形容词不能直接当主语，前面加 being，就变成「这件事」。
Being honest is hard.
Being honest is the only way to feel better.
… being kind is one of the best ways …
⚠️ 主语是一件事，后面的动词用单数：Being kind makes you happy.
satisfaction生词n. 满足感，满意
想要的得到了、事情做成了以后，那种「够了、值了」的感觉。
Mia watched the happy faces with deep satisfaction.
Every small kindness gives her a little satisfaction.
⚠️ 说人「感到满意」用 satisfied：I'm satisfied，不说 I'm satisfying。
bliss生词n. 极大的幸福
幸福到了顶点，一点烦恼都没有，比 happiness 强得多。
This is pure bliss!
… sometimes even a moment of bliss.
⚠️ 不可数：It was bliss，不说 a bliss。

换成你自己的事

每题说一句，说出声；卡住了再看参考
1. 用 It turns out that，说一件「本来以为……结果发现……」的事。
看参考说法
I thought grammar was boring. It turns out that it is like a puzzle.
2. 用 Being + 形容词，说一句你相信的道理。
看参考说法
Being patient is the key to learning English.
3. 用 keep … at bay，说说你怎么对付压力。
看参考说法
I go for a walk every evening to keep stress at bay.
4. 用 beat yourself up，安慰一个做错事的朋友。
看参考说法
Don't beat yourself up. You did your best.
5. 用 take responsibility for，说一件你自己负责的事。
看参考说法
I take responsibility for my own learning.
6. 用 satisfaction 或 bliss，说一个让你特别满足、特别幸福的时刻。
看参考说法
Lying in bed on a rainy Sunday morning is pure bliss.
