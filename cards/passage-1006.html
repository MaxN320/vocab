The Rainy Night

雨夜
先自己读一遍，黄色的是生词本和词组里的词，点一下能听发音。读不懂的段落，点「中文」看翻译。最后点「🔊 从头听一遍」，边听边跟着读。
　全部中文：
Lily's father was a truck driver. He took great pride in his work and always drove as well as possible. But Lily didn't get along with him. She was obsessed with her phone, and she spent every weekend hanging out with her friends. "You're never home, Dad," she often said.
莉莉的爸爸是个卡车司机。他对自己的工作非常自豪，开车总是尽心尽力。可莉莉和他处不来。她痴迷手机，每个周末都和朋友在外面玩。「爸，你从来都不在家。」她常这么说。
One rainy night, a message popped up on her phone. It was from the police: "Your father's truck went off the road and fell into the river. He nearly drowned. He needs surgery now." The message was short, but it was not ambiguous at all. Lily was extremely scared.
一个下雨的夜里，她手机上突然弹出一条消息，是警察发来的：「你父亲的卡车冲出了公路，掉进了河里。他差点淹死，现在需要马上做手术。」消息很短，却一点也不含糊。莉莉吓坏了。
At the hospital, the doctor urged her to stay calm. "The surgery went well," he said. "But your father needs adequate rest. Make sure he takes two of these tablets every day." Then Lily went to collect her father's things. In his bag, she came across a pile of letters. They were all to her, but he had never sent them.
在医院，医生劝她冷静。「手术很顺利，」他说，「不过你父亲需要充分休息。记得让他每天吃两片这种药。」随后，莉莉去取爸爸的东西。在他的包里，她无意中发现了一沓信。信全是写给她的，可他一封也没寄出去。
One sentence appeared many times: "My daughter is the greatest adventure of my life." Lily read it again and again, and she began to reflect on the way she had treated him. She could not remember the last time she had paid him a compliment.
有一句话出现了很多次：「我女儿是我这辈子最了不起的冒险。」莉莉读了一遍又一遍，开始反思自己是怎么对待爸爸的。她想不起来，自己上一次夸他是什么时候了。
A week later, Lily picked her father up from the hospital. That evening, she stepped out of her comfort zone. She turned down the volume of her music, put her phone away, and cooked dinner for him for the first time. The kitchen smelled warm and inviting. "Dad, I'm proud of you," she said. It was a small thing, but for Lily it felt like a real accomplishment.
一周后，莉莉去医院接爸爸回家。那天晚上，她走出了自己的舒适区：把音乐的音量调小，把手机收起来，第一次给爸爸做了晚饭。厨房里暖暖的，香气诱人。「爸，我为你骄傲。」她说。这是件小事，但对莉莉来说，却像完成了一件了不起的事。
From that day on, they shared the housework equally, and they got along better than ever.
从那天起，他们平均分担家务，父女俩相处得比以往任何时候都好。
