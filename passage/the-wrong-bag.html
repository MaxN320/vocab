The Wrong Bag

I was in a hurry after work. I picked up a black bag by the door and ran to the bus stop. My bus was already there, but my keys weren’t in the front pocket. I opened the bag. Inside were two apples and a pink lunch box. My lunch box was blue.

I ran back to the office. Anna was still by the door, holding my bag. Hers was just as old as mine. Even the broken zip was the same.

“How did you know it was mine?” I asked.

She held up my lunch box. “My mum doesn’t write my name on my lunch anymore.”

闭卷说一句：她的包和我的包一样旧。（用 as + 形容词 + as）
