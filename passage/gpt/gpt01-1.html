Her brother Leo recommended/ˌrekəˈmendɪd/ a grocery store two blocks/blɒks/ from home. Mina was in a hurry; it was almost/ˈɔːlməʊst/ eleven. She made sure she had her list and bought bread, fruit, and a snack/snæk/ for each person. The bread there was not as expensive as the bread near the park.
哥哥利奥推荐了一家离家两个街区的杂货店。米娜赶时间，快十一点了。她确认带了清单，买了面包、水果和每人一份小吃。那里的面包没有公园附近的贵。
At home, she wrapped/ræpt/ the sandwiches and put them in a bag. The fruit was already/ɔːlˈredi/ in it. Leo called. "I can pick you up at eleven thirty." "Thanks. I'm ready now," Mina said.

At the park, they came across an old friend by the gate. "Eat with us!" Mina said. They arrived in time for lunch. A crowd/kraʊd/ sat at the tables, but Leo spotted/ˈspɒtɪd/ a quiet place under a tree.
在公园门口，他们偶然遇到一位老朋友。「和我们一起吃吧！」米娜说。他们赶上了午餐时间。桌子都被一群人占了，但利奥发现树下有个安静的地方。
The wind/wɪnd/ lifted their blanket/ˈblæŋkɪt/. Leo held/held/ one corner while Mina set out the food. "This picnic is as good as a big party," her sister said. Mina smiled. "I really appreciate/əˈpriːʃieɪt/ your help, Leo."
风把他们的野餐毯掀了起来。利奥按住一角，米娜摆好食物。妹妹说：「这次野餐和大聚会一样棒。」「利奥，真的谢谢你帮忙。」米娜笑了。

拆开看：这 5 个

留意同一个说法在故事里怎么变形
To do, S + V骨架为了做……，某人做了……
句首先说目的，后面再说行动；省钱和做午餐的是同一个人。
To save money, she decided to make lunch herself.
To find a gift within her budget, she looked at used books.（第 3 篇）
⚠️ 句首 To save money 回答『为什么自己做』，不是另一个人的目的。
(not) as + 形容词 + as骨架（不）和……一样……
拿两家店的面包比较价格；最后把野餐和聚会比较。
The bread was not as expensive as the bread near the park.
This picnic is as good as a big party.
⚠️ 两处 as 中间用原级 expensive / good，不说 as more expensive as。
in a hurry / in time词组赶时间 / 及时赶上
买东西时赶，最后还是赶上了午餐；前者是状态，后者是结果。
Mina was in a hurry.
They arrived in time for lunch.
⚠️ in time 是来得及；on time 才是按约定时刻准点。
pick sb up词组接人
利奥来接米娜，不是『捡起米娜』。
I can pick you up at eleven thirty.
She needed to pick up her little brother from school at four.（第 3 篇）
⚠️ 代词放中间：pick you up；不说 pick up you。
budget / afford单词预算 / 负担得起
钱的总额度有限，所以不去餐厅。
Her budget was small.
She could not afford to eat at a restaurant.
⚠️ afford 后常接 to do：can't afford to eat out。
