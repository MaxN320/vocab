　全部中文：
Saturday, 10 p.m. It has been a week since I moved into this/ðɪs/ flat, and I'm still not sure whether I chose the right place. When I arrived, I didn't/ˈdɪdnt/ know/nəʊ/ a single person in/ɪn/ the city and had/hæd/ nobody to talk to, so/səʊ/ I spent the first night wondering what/wɒt/ I would do if something went wrong.
星期六，晚上十点。我搬进这套公寓已经一个星期了，可还是说不准自己是不是选对了地方。刚到的时候，我在这座城市一个人都不认识，也没有人可以说话，所以第一个晚上我一直在想：要是出了什么事，我该怎么办。
That was before I met Tom, the man who lives next door and who is responsible for the whole building. If he hadn't been in the hall that morning, carrying a box that looked far too heavy for/fɔː(r)/ one person, I might never have found the courage to say/seɪ/ hello. "Are you new here?" he asked, in a voice so warm that/ðæt/ I forgot to be shy.
那是在我认识汤姆之前。他就住在隔壁，整栋楼也归他管。要不是那天早上他正好在楼道里，搬着一个一个人根本搬不动的大箱子，我可能永远鼓不起勇气跟他打招呼。「你是新搬来的吗？」他问，声音那么温和，我一下子就忘了害羞。
What I like/laɪk/ most about him is not that he helps people, but the way he does/dʌz/ it: quietly, without making them feel like they owe him anything. When I lost my key on/ɒn/ Monday, he didn't just/dʒʌst/ give me a spare one; he sat with me on the stairs until I felt well/wel/ enough to go/ɡəʊ/ in. I wish I knew how to thank him.
我最喜欢他的地方，不是他帮人，而是他帮人的方式：安安静静的，从不让人觉得欠了他什么。星期一我把钥匙弄丢了，他不只是给了我一把备用的，还陪我在楼梯上坐着，直到我缓过来、能进屋了。真希望我知道该怎么谢谢他。
There are so many things I have to learn at the hospital, such as where every medicine is kept and which doctor works on which floor, that sometimes I think/θɪŋk/ I will/wɪl/ never be good enough. But Tom says/sez/ nobody gets/ɡets/ everything right in their first week, and that being slow at the start has nothing to do with being a bad nurse. What is going on in my head, he says, is just fear, not the truth.
在医院要学的东西太多了，比如每种药放在哪儿、哪位医生在哪一层上班，多得让我有时候觉得自己永远都不够好。可汤姆说，谁也不可能第一个星期就把所有事做对；刚开始慢一点，跟是不是好护士没有关系。他说，我脑子里乱糟糟的那些，不过是害怕，不是事实。
Tomorrow I'm going to visit his sister Anna, who has invited me to go swimming with her. Had someone told me a week ago that I would have friends here by Saturday, I wouldn't have believed it. For the first time since I came, I'm not afraid of Monday. Well, maybe only a little.
明天我打算去拜访他妹妹安娜，她约我跟她一起去游泳。要是一个星期前有人告诉我，到了星期六我在这儿就会有朋友，我才不会信。来这儿以后，我第一次不害怕星期一了。好吧，也许还有一点点。

难句拆解：7 句

先找主干，再看挂在主干上的从句、不定式和分词
It has been a week since I moved into this flat, and I'm still not sure whether I chose the right place.完成时 + whether 从句我搬进这套公寓已经一个星期了，可还是说不准自己是不是选对了地方。
两句话用 and 连起来：It has been a week … ｜ I'm still not sure whether …
It has been + 一段时间 + since + 过去式：自从……已经有多久了
I'm not sure whether …：不确定是不是……；whether 后面用陈述语序
⚠️ since 后面用过去式（since I moved），主句用现在完成时（It has been）。
When I arrived, I didn't know a single person in the city, so I spent the first night wondering what I would do if something went wrong.分词 + 宾语从句 + 虚拟刚到的时候，我在这座城市一个人都不认识，所以第一个晚上我一直在想：要是出了事，我该怎么办。
主干：I spent the first night wondering …
spend + 时间 + doing：花时间做某事
what I would do：宾语从句，陈述语序
if something went wrong：用过去式说「万一」，跟着 would do
⚠️ spend 后面接 doing，不接 to do。
If he hadn't been in the hall that morning, carrying a box that looked far too heavy for one person, I might never have found the courage to say hello.虚拟（过去）+ 分词 + 定语从句要不是那天早上他正好在楼道里，搬着一个一个人根本搬不动的大箱子，我可能永远鼓不起勇气跟他打招呼。
主干：If he hadn't been in the hall, I might never have found the courage.
If + had done …, might / would have done：说过去没发生的事和它的结果
carrying a box：现在分词短语，说他当时在干什么
a box that looked far too heavy：that 引导的定语从句，修饰 box
the courage to say hello：不定式修饰 courage，说是什么勇气
⚠️ if 那一半用 had done，结果那一半用 would / might have done，两半都往过去推一格。
What I like most about him is not that he helps people, but the way he does it.主语从句 + not … but …我最喜欢他的地方，不是他帮人，而是他帮人的方式。
主干：What I like most … is not A, but B.
What I like most about him：what 引导的主语从句，整块当主语
not that he helps people, but the way he does it：不是 A，而是 B
⚠️ what 从句当主语时，后面的 be 动词用单数：What I like most is …
I wish I knew how to thank him.虚拟（现在）+ 疑问词 + 不定式真希望我知道该怎么谢谢他。
I wish + 过去式：对现在的遗憾（其实现在不知道）。
I wish I knew：现在的事用过去式，表示「可惜现在不是这样」
how to thank him：疑问词 + 不定式 = 该怎么……
⚠️ 对现在的遗憾用过去式（I wish I knew），对过去的遗憾用 had done（I wish I had known）。
There are so many things I have to learn at the hospital, such as where every medicine is kept, that sometimes I think I will never be good enough.so … that + 被动 + 定语从句在医院要学的东西太多了，比如每种药放在哪儿，多得让我有时候觉得自己永远都不够好。
主干：There are so many things … that …
so many + 名词 + that …：多得……以至于……
things I have to learn：省掉了 that 的定语从句
where every medicine is kept：被动（be + 过去分词），药「被放」在哪儿
⚠️ so 后面接 many / much + 名词或直接接形容词；such 后面接 a / an + 名词。
Had someone told me a week ago that I would have friends here by Saturday, I wouldn't have believed it.虚拟倒装要是一个星期前有人告诉我，到了星期六我在这儿就会有朋友，我才不会信。
= If someone had told me … , I wouldn't have believed it.
Had someone told me …：去掉 if，把 had 提到主语前面，意思不变
that I would have friends here by Saturday：宾语从句，站在过去说将来用 would
I wouldn't have believed it：过去没发生的结果
⚠️ 能这样倒装的，只有 had、were、should 开头的虚拟句。

换成你自己的事

每题说一句，说出声；卡住了再看参考
1. 用 It has been … since …，说你做某件事多久了。
看参考说法
It has been two years since I started learning English.
2. 用 If I had …, I would have …，说一件你后悔的事。
看参考说法
If I had gone to bed earlier, I wouldn't have been so tired today.
3. 用 I wish I + 过去式，说一个对现在的遗憾。
看参考说法
I wish I had more time to read.
4. 用 What I like most about … is …，夸一个人。
看参考说法
What I like most about my friend is that he always listens.
