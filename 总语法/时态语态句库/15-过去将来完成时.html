过去将来完成时

站在过去看：到将来某个截止点，已经做完了。

看讲解：时态篇 15 →语态篇 →
信号词said / thought + would have done + by …
橙 谓语第一个词：扛时间，否定加 not、疑问提前都靠它蓝 时态的其余部分紫 被动：be + done绿 疑问词
公式要点A 主动↔被动B 只有主动

公式

主动
陈述主语 + would + have done
否定主语 + wouldn't + have done
一般疑问Would + 主语 + have done？
特殊疑问疑问词 + would + 主语 + have done？
被动
陈述主语 + would + have been done
否定主语 + wouldn't + have been done
一般疑问Would + 主语 + have been done？
特殊疑问疑问词 + would + 主语 + have been done？

do＝动词原形　doing＝现在分词　done＝过去分词　疑问词＝when / where / why / how / what …

要点

  • would have done 也是虚拟语气的样子：If I had known, I would have told you.（虚拟，同形不同用）

A · 主动 ↔ 被动4 组

同一件事说两遍：先让做事的人当主语（主动），再让被做的东西当主语（被动）。每边四句：陈述 → 否定 → 一般疑问 → 特殊疑问。

A01finishfinished · finished完成
主动
陈述
He said they would have finished the work by June.
他说到六月他们就会把活干完。
否定
He said they wouldn't have finished the work by June.
一般疑问
Would they have finished the work by June? Nobody knew.
特殊疑问
How much of the work would they have finished by June? Nobody knew.
到六月他们能干完多少？谁也不知道。
被动
陈述
He said the work would have been finished by June.
他说活到六月就会干完。
否定
He said the work wouldn't have been finished by June.
一般疑问
Would the work have been finished by June? Nobody knew.
特殊疑问
How much of the work would have been finished by June? Nobody knew.
到六月能干完多少？谁也不知道。
A02repairrepaired · repaired修理
主动
陈述
She told me they would have repaired my car by the time I got back.
她告诉我，等我回来时他们就会把车修好。
否定
She told me they wouldn't have repaired my car by the time I got back.
一般疑问
Would they have repaired my car by the time I got back? I wasn't sure.
被动
陈述
She told me my car would have been repaired by the time I got back.
她告诉我，等我回来时车就修好了。
否定
She told me my car wouldn't have been repaired by the time I got back.
一般疑问
Would my car have been repaired by the time I got back? I wasn't sure.
A03sellsold · sold卖
主动
陈述
We thought they would have sold the tickets by noon.
我们以为到中午他们就会把票卖完。
否定
We thought they wouldn't have sold the tickets by noon.
一般疑问
Would they have sold the tickets by noon? We had no idea.
特殊疑问
How many tickets would they have sold by noon? We had no idea.
到中午他们会卖出多少张票？我们心里一点数都没有。
被动
陈述
We thought the tickets would have been sold by noon.
我们以为到中午票就会卖完。
否定
We thought the tickets wouldn't have been sold by noon.
一般疑问
Would the tickets have been sold by noon? We had no idea.
特殊疑问
How many tickets would have been sold by noon? We had no idea.
到中午会卖出多少张票？我们心里一点数都没有。

B · 只有主动的高频句2 组

日常最常说的句子：主语就是做事的人，或者动词后面没有宾语，只练主动四句。

B01leaveleft · left离开
主动
陈述
She thought he would have left by then.
她以为那时他已经走了。
否定
She thought he wouldn't have left by then.
一般疑问
Would he have left by then? She wasn't sure.
B02finishfinished · finished完成
主动
陈述
I hoped I would have finished the book by Friday.
我原本希望周五前能把书看完。
否定
I was afraid I wouldn't have finished the book by Friday.
一般疑问
Would I have finished the book by Friday? I wasn't sure.
