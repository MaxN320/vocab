怎么用

  1. 先看页头的公式：谓语第一个词（橙色）是谁，后面跟什么。
  2. 读卡片时，先读两句陈述句：主动句的主语是做事的人，被动句的主语是被做的东西。
  3. 再往下读否定、一般疑问、特殊疑问：只看橙色那个词怎么动——后面加 not，或者提到主语前面。
  4. 熟了以后，遮住英文，看中文先自己说一遍，再对答案。

四句怎么变：只动第一个词

  • 否定：谓语第一个词后面加 not。They have cleaned → They haven't cleaned.
  • 一般疑问：把第一个词提到主语前面。Have they cleaned the room?　简答也用回它：Yes, they have.
  • 特殊疑问：疑问词放最前，后面照一般疑问句的语序。How many rooms have they cleaned?
  • 没有助动词：（一般现在、一般过去的主动句）请 do / does / did 来当第一个词，动词回原形。They cleaned → They didn't clean → Did they clean?
  • 被动句不借 do：被动句里本来就有 be，它就是第一个词。Was the room cleaned?（✗ Did the room cleaned?）
  • 问主语不倒装：Who cleaned the room?　被动句问主语也一样：What was cleaned?
  • 被动＝be + done：be 按时态变：is done · was done · will be done · is being done · has been done · had been done · will have been done · would be done · would have been done。将来进行时和 4 个完成进行时没有被动。

有助动词：现在完成时

例1cleancleaned · cleaned打扫
主动
陈述
They have already cleaned the room.
他们已经把房间打扫好了。
否定
They haven't cleaned the room yet.
一般疑问
Have they cleaned the room yet?
Yes, they have. / No, they haven't.
特殊疑问
How many rooms have they cleaned?
他们打扫了几个房间？
被动
陈述
The room has already been cleaned.
房间已经打扫好了。
否定
The room hasn't been cleaned yet.
一般疑问
Has the room been cleaned yet?
Yes, it has. / No, it hasn't.
特殊疑问
How many rooms have been cleaned?
打扫了几个房间？

没有助动词：一般过去时（主动借 did，被动不借）

例2cleancleaned · cleaned打扫
主动
陈述
They cleaned the room yesterday.
他们昨天打扫了房间。
否定
They didn't clean the room yesterday.
一般疑问
Did they clean the room yesterday?
Yes, they did. / No, they didn't.
特殊疑问
When did they clean the room?
他们什么时候打扫的房间？
被动
陈述
The room was cleaned yesterday.
房间昨天打扫了。
否定
The room wasn't cleaned yesterday.
一般疑问
Was the room cleaned yesterday?
Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.
特殊疑问
When was the room cleaned?
房间是什么时候打扫的？

一句话走遍 16 个时态

同一件事：他们打扫房间。只换时间状语和谓语，看主动、被动各长什么样。

一般现在时每天（常态）
主动They clean the room every day.
被动The room is cleaned every day.
现在进行时现在正在
主动They are cleaning the room now.
被动The room is being cleaned now.
现在完成时已经（结果在现在）
主动They have already cleaned the room.
被动The room has already been cleaned.
现在完成进行时一直在做，到现在
主动They have been cleaning the room for an hour.
被动没有 · 改用 has been cleaned
一般过去时昨天（过去）
主动They cleaned the room yesterday.
被动The room was cleaned yesterday.
过去进行时昨晚九点正在
主动They were cleaning the room at nine last night.
被动The room was being cleaned at nine last night.
过去完成时过去的过去
主动They had cleaned the room before we arrived.
被动The room had been cleaned before we arrived.
过去完成进行时到过去某点，一直在做
主动They had been cleaning the room for an hour when we arrived.
被动没有 · 改用 had been cleaned
一般将来时明天（将来）
主动They will clean the room tomorrow.
被动The room will be cleaned tomorrow.
将来进行时明天九点正在
主动They will be cleaning the room at nine tomorrow.
被动没有 · 改用 will be cleaned
将来完成时到将来某点，已经做完
主动They will have cleaned the room by noon tomorrow.
被动The room will have been cleaned by noon tomorrow.
将来完成进行时到将来某点，一直在做
主动They will have been cleaning the room for two hours by noon tomorrow.
被动没有 · 改用 will have been cleaned
一般过去将来时过去看将来
主动He said they would clean the room the next day.
被动He said the room would be cleaned the next day.
过去将来进行时过去看将来那一刻正在
主动He said they would be cleaning the room at nine the next day.
被动没有 · 改用 would be cleaned
过去将来完成时过去看将来，到时已做完
主动He said they would have cleaned the room by noon the next day.
被动He said the room would have been cleaned by noon the next day.
过去将来完成进行时过去看将来，到时一直在做
主动He said they would have been cleaning the room for two hours by noon the next day.
被动没有 · 改用 would have been cleaned
